### Added

- New module `coilix-pekko`: a Pekko `ActorSystem` and a `Materializer` as the beans `actorSystem` and `materializer`, which beans receive like any dependency, also as `using` parameters. The default dispatcher runs on the `TaskExecutor`, the internal dispatcher is the same dispatcher, and `pekko.actor.default-blocking-io-dispatcher` runs on the `BlockingExecutor`, so Pekko starts no thread pools of its own. The name, `coilix.pekko.shutdown-timeout` (30 s) and the configuration of Pekko come from `coilix.pekko`, without a separate `application.conf`, and Pekko logs through SLF4J. The context stops the `ActorSystem` with a coordinated shutdown after the beans that use it; Pekko registers no shutdown hook and does not end the JVM, and an `ActorSystem` that stops by itself goes to the `FatalErrorHandler` as `ActorSystemStoppedException` (COILIX-00029).
