### Added

- New modules `coilix-processing` and `coilix-processing-annotations`: background processors. A class with `@Processor` that extends `BlockingProcessor` or `CustomProcessor` is created with its dependencies by the `processorManager` bean, not registered as a bean. Its work is split into shards (`shards` under `coilix.processors.<name>`, or `defaultShards`) over fixed slots (`ShardKeys.slotOf`), and the shards of this replica, chosen by the `round-robin`, `range`, `pinned` or `all` assignment, run on virtual threads named `coilix-processor-<name>-<shard>`. A failed shard restarts alone with backoff and becomes `Failed` after `max-attempts` within `within`, counting again after `reset-after` of stable work. `ProcessorManager` starts, stops and restarts processors and single shards and reports their status and topology, and the shards stop when the context closes, within `coilix.processors.shutdown-timeout` (COILIX-00026).
