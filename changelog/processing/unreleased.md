### Added

- New modules `coilix-processing` and `coilix-processing-annotations`: background processors. A class with `@Processor` that extends `BlockingProcessor` or `CustomProcessor` is created with its dependencies by the `processorManager` bean, not registered as a bean. Its work is split into shards (`shards` under `coilix.processors.<name>`, or `defaultShards`) over fixed slots (`ShardKeys.slotOf`), and the shards of this replica, chosen by the `round-robin`, `range`, `pinned` or `all` assignment, run on virtual threads named `coilix-processor-<name>-<shard>`. A failed shard restarts alone with backoff and becomes `Failed` after `max-attempts` within `within`, counting again after `reset-after` of stable work. `ProcessorManager` starts, stops and restarts processors and single shards and reports their status and topology, and the shards stop when the context closes, within `coilix.processors.shutdown-timeout` (COILIX-00026).
- `StatelessProcessor[T]` reads the `InputSource[T]` of each shard in a loop, calls `process` for each element in order and acknowledges it afterwards. A failing element ends the worker with `ElementFailedException`, and after the restart the element comes again with a higher delivery number (`ElementContext.delivery`). `InputSource`, `Input`, `InputId`, `Acknowledgement` and `Output` let connectors plug in without a stream library, and `InMemoryStream` (with slots) and `InMemoryOutput` work in memory. A source with its own `slotCount` needs the same `slots` for the processor (COILIX-00026).
- `ProcessorManager.statistics(id)` returns the state and the counters of each shard of this replica: processed and failed elements, total and longest processing time, last processed element and the lag of each slot, kept across restarts in `Shard.metrics` (COILIX-00026).
- The processor manager publishes `ProcessorEvent.ShardStateChanged` for every change of the state of a shard and `ProcessorEvent.ShardFailed` for every failure of a worker through the `EventPublisher` (COILIX-00026).
