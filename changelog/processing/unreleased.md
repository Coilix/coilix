### Added

- New modules `coilix-processing` and `coilix-processing-annotations`: background processors. A class with `@Processor` that extends `IOProcessor` is created with its dependencies by the `processorManager` bean, not registered as a bean. Its work is split into shards (`shards` under `coilix.processors.<name>`, or `defaultShards`) over fixed slots (`ShardKeys.slotOf`), and the shards of this replica, chosen by the `round-robin`, `range`, `pinned` or `all` assignment, run as fibers of the cats-effect runtime; `prepare`, `onAcquired`, `onReleased` and the work return `IO[Unit]`, and a shard that does not stop within half of the stop timeout is cancelled (COILIX-00032). A failed shard restarts alone with backoff and becomes `Failed` after `max-attempts` within `within`, counting again after `reset-after` of stable work. `ProcessorManager` starts, stops and restarts processors, in `IO`, and single shards and reports their status and topology, and the shards stop when the context closes, within `coilix.processors.shutdown-timeout` (COILIX-00026).
- `StreamProcessor[A]` describes the work of a shard as an fs2 `source` and a `flow` (`Pipe`). Stopping a shard ends the source right after the next element, and the elements already read go on through the flow, so a batch waiting in `groupWithin` is still saved. `drain-timeout` of a processor (also under `defaults`) limits how long the work may run after the stop signal before it is cancelled, by default half of the stop timeout, for `IOProcessor` too, and `Shard.drainTimeout` carries it (COILIX-00032).
