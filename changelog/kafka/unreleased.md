### Added

- New module `coilix-kafka` on fs2-kafka with a client for each Kafka cluster under `coilix.kafka.clients.<name>`: bootstrap servers, client id, SASL and SSL credentials read as secrets, consumer and producer settings with other Kafka properties by name, batched commits and admin settings. The `kafkaClients` bean (`KafkaClients`) finds a client by name or by the client of a topic, and throws `NoSuchKafkaClientException` with the configured names when it is missing. `KafkaTopic[K, V]` carries the codecs of keys and values (`TopicCodec`, given for `String`, `Array[Byte]`, `Int`, `Long` and `UUID`), its client and the settings it is created with. A `KafkaClient` publishes with `publish`, `publishAll` and `publishing`, reads with `subscribe` (a consumer group) and `assign` (chosen partitions) or a raw `consumer`, and commits offsets in batches with `commits`; an undecodable record fails the stream with `RecordDecodingException`. Its `KafkaAdmin` creates topics, reads partitions, earliest, latest and committed offsets, moves the offsets of a group and deletes groups, with a timeout and a warning for slow operations. Producers, consumers and admin clients close with the context (COILIX-00032).
