### Added

- New module `coilix-cats-effect`: the `IORuntime` of the application as the `ioRuntime` bean, with the work-stealing pool of cats-effect sized by `coilix.concurrency.compute.threads`, shut down with the context. Its compute pool becomes the only compute pool of the application: the `ioTaskExecutor` bean is the `TaskExecutor`, so the default pool of the concurrency module is not created. A shutdown of the runtime after a fatal error in a fiber goes to the `FatalErrorHandler` as `IORuntimeStoppedException`, and `IO.onBlockingExecutor` runs a blocking call on a virtual thread of the `BlockingExecutor` (COILIX-00020).
- `@Bean` methods may return `IO[A]` or `Resource[IO, A]`: other beans receive the value `A`. The beans are created with one `unsafeRunSync` on the `ioRuntime` bean, independent ones at the same time, level by level, and resources close in the reverse order when the context closes, each finalizer within `coilix.cats-effect.shutdown-timeout` (30 s) or its `@ShutdownTimeout`; a slow finalizer is cancelled and a failing one logged, and closing goes on. A failing effect closes the resources opened before it and stops the startup. A lazy or prototype bean made by an effect stops the startup with `InvalidEffectfulBeanException` (COILIX-00020).
